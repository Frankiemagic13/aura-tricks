Hold the share button for 1 second to open this menu. Hold the ••• button or the date for 1 second to clear the note.

Open and close

Hold the share button for 1 second to open these settings. Hold ••• (or the date) for 1 second to clear the note.

Peeks

Each word goes to your PeekSmith (live peek), to PeekLink / Peek Island (online) and to your iPhone with Aura peer. Drawings go to the PeekSmith screen.

Buttons

Aa (keyboard bar): double tap = prediction with the selected preset · double tap again while it runs = next preset
Paperclip (bottom bar and keyboard bar): double tap = swap Spectator ↔ Magician (both devices)
Table (keyboard bar): double tap = mirror on/off (both devices; on = this phone becomes the Magician)
Share: hold 1 s = these settings
•••: hold 1 s = clear the note (the date does the same)
Checklist (bottom bar and keyboard bar): double tap = start the Acrostic. This phone becomes the Magician, and the mirror goes off.
Markup: draw (drawings go to the PeekSmith)
New note: clears the note
Undo: undo the last change
Back: no secret function. The keyboard bar shows when you tap the note.

Prediction (Aa)

  1. Add your presets in the Predictions tab (for example "You're thinking of", "I knew you'd write"). Tap ● to select one.
  2. Tap your note, then double tap Aa. The prediction starts on YOUR phone (it becomes the Magician, the mirror goes off). Double tap Aa again to go to the next preset.
  3. The spectator types. Your note shows the preset + their word (first letter small; case from "Case of their word").
  4. It stops after Return or the stop time. The text stays on your screen.

Acrostic (checklist)

  1. Double tap the checklist. The top shows "Swipe to input number".
  2. Swipe up or down above the toolbars: each swipe = +1 and a buzz (1 = first letter, up to 5). The page does not move.
  3. The spectator types a word. Your note shows common words; the letters at your position spell their word. It stops by itself.

Drawings

Their drawing shows on your phone a little changed (turned, moved, other line), as if a different hand drew it. Set "Exact copy" in the Predictions tab for a true copy.

Setup

Your iPhone: Aura Browser, Magician. Spectator's device: AuraClip, Spectator. Same room on the two devices (default fvt-calc). Keep the two pages on screen.
