Bluetooth from the spectator's phone directly to your iPhone. No internet is necessary. Your iPhone: open this page in Aura Browser and set "This phone" to "My phone (mirror)" (or add ?mirror=1 to the link). Your Notes then shows the spectator's note and drawings live, and the peeks go to your PeekSmith from your phone. The spectator's phone stays "Spectator". Use the same room on the two phones (default fvt-calc, the same as the calculator and PeekLink). Keep the two pages open and on screen.
Prediction (on your phone, as Magician): hold the back button for 1 second. The spectator's note changes to the prediction text. Then everything you type on your phone shows on the spectator's phone after that text. Hold the back button again to stop: the normal mirror starts again. Prediction case "Swap": each capital letter you type shows as a small letter on the spectator's phone, and each small letter as a capital.
