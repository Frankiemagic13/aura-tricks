Auto connect: the page connects to the Cosmos when it opens and connects again if the connection is lost (it tries every 20 s). If you tap Disconnect, it waits until you tap Connect Cosmos. Close the Cosmos app first. Works in Aura Browser.
Print when typing stops: the Cosmos prints when the spectator stops typing for that time. It prints each note one time; it prints again only if the note changes.
Feed trigger: pushes the paper out (the same as the Feed button).
Print speed: Slow and Slower send the dark rows more slowly, so the letters can print darker (a print takes 1–4 s more). Speed test print: one slip with your template's font and size 3 times (1 Normal, 2 Slow, 3 Slower), to compare.
Print trigger: prints the template you select under Cosmos template. A volume or PeekSmith trigger replaces what that button did before (you also see it in the Volume and Peeks tabs). Volume buttons do nothing while the settings are open.

Drawings

  
Typed word → picture
Their drawing on my phone

  
Your phone shows the spectator's drawing as if a different hand drew it. Random style: each new drawing gets one of 13 styles (sketch, crayon, colored pencils, ballpoint, chalk, mirror…). Or select one style, or Exact copy. Typed word → picture: about 600 words have a drawing (animals, food, things at home, toys, clothes, nature…). Colors: each closed shape gets its own color. The PeekSmith gets the same drawing.
Typed word → picture: while your phone (Magician) is on the drawing page, a word they type (house, cat, heart, car, star… about 70 words) is drawn on your canvas line by line, in the drawing style. Your note does not show their text.

Experimental. Tap Test: the camera opens (front by default). Hold the word in the yellow box. The page waits 1.5 s, takes one picture and reads it. Tap Test again to read again. Notes v37



  
Fake lock

  
Volume tab: set a volume button to "Fake lock". One press: the lock sound plays (if on) and the screen goes black, like a locked phone. Hold the black screen: what the spectator wrote shows while you hold. Lift your finger: black again. Press the volume button again, or triple tap (if on): the note comes back. If the spectator deletes the note (all or part), the peek still shows what they wrote before they deleted it ("Peek deleted text", on by default). The "Fake lock" card in the Volume tab sets the sound and how the peek looks, with a preview.

  
Text recognition (experimental)

  
Experimental tab: choose the camera (front or back) and tap Test. Hold the word in the yellow box. After 1.5 s the page reads it and shows the best word and all the text. Tap Stop camera when you are done (closing the settings stops it too).

  
Open and close

  
Hold the share button for 1 second to open these settings. Hold ••• (or the date) for 1 second to clear the note.

  
Notes API

  
API tab: other apps can get what the spectator typed. Tap Copy URL and paste it in the other app (Value: text, Method: GET). Reset key makes a new URL; the old one stops at once. Clear saved note after a show.

  
Peeks

  
Each word goes to your PeekSmith (live peek), to PeekLink / Peek Island (online) and to your iPhone with Aura peer. Drawings go to the PeekSmith screen.
