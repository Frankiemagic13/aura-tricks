Printer resolution
Calibration print: a frame as wide as the print line, a ruler (a mark each mm, numbers each 10 mm) and a square. Measure the square: it must be 20 mm. If it is about 13 mm, choose 300 dpi. If it is about 30 mm, choose 203 dpi. Then do the calibration print again.

Auto print: while the page is on screen, it checks Inject every 1.5 s. When a new word arrives, it prints the current setup with that word at once and sends "Printed: word" to the PeekSmith. Words that were already in Inject before you turned it on do not print. If the Cosmos is not connected, the page connects first. {prediction} is a placeholder. When you tap Print, the page gets the word from the source and prints it in its place. Test prints the sample word. The fallback word prints when the source has no word. "Only a new Inject word" waits (up to 60 s) for a word sent after you tap Print.

Only in Aura Browser. Close the PeekSmith app first. The Cosmos and the PeekSmith can be connected at the same time.

1. Tap the Cosmos pill to connect the printer. The PeekSmith connects by itself (Settings → PeekSmith).

2. Type the text. Tap { } Prediction to put the prediction at the cursor. Tap { } Notes to put what is written on the Notes page (it prints from the Notes page; here the preview shows "Your note").

3. Paper length: Fixed keeps the paper at one length (61 mm is the usual slip). Auto makes the paper as long as the text.

4. Drag the text on the paper, or use the arrows (1 mm each). Center puts it back in the middle.

5. Tap a font (✍️ = your own handwriting font, see Settings → My fonts). Use A−, A+ or the slider under the fonts to change the text size (each tap = 0.5 mm). More settings are in Settings → Handwriting.

6. Print gets the prediction word and prints. Test prints the sample word.

Routines (grid icon): save the current setup with a name, tap one to load it. Edit lets you rename, update (save the current setup into it) or delete. Routines also show on the Notes and Calculator pages.
