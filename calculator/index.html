Calculator v51

Open and close

Hold 0 for 1 second to open these settings. Tap Done to close.

Force (4th number)

  1. Spectators type numbers with + between them (default: 3 numbers).
  2. The last person taps + and types "any" fourth number. Each digit they tap shows the next digit of the necessary number.
  3. They tap =. The total is the force: the date and time (1 minute ahead) or your custom number.

Quick force: type a number, then hold 1 for 1 second. Battery: type 1–100, then hold 8. AC makes the force ready again.

Codebreaker

Hold = to turn it on or off. After an equation, tap C / AC, then each = shows the numbers of that equation one by one (default: last number first). Settings and steps are in the Codebreaker tab.

PIN math

Hold +/− (bottom left) to turn it on or off ("PIN on" / "PIN off" shows at the top), or use PIN peek on = in the Code tab. It turns off by itself after one =. The spectator adds birth year + age + the time + their PIN and taps =. The PeekSmith shows "PIN 4820/4821": the first PIN is correct when the birthday has passed, the second when it has not.

Vanish & reveal

  1. Connect the two phones with Aura peer (Peeks tab). Your phone is My phone (mirror). "Status" in the Vanish tab shows Linked.
  2. Type a number on your phone (or show a result).
  3. Swipe across the display. The number goes away and your phone shows 0. The PeekSmith shows "Sent 1,234 · 3 s".
  4. After the delay, the number comes into view on the spectator's phone with the reveal animation.

One digit per swipe: each swipe takes one digit (1234 comes back as 4, 34, 234, 1234). This also works on a result after =.

More than one number: with "One number per swipe", each swipe takes one number (default: the last number first). The number comes into view on their phone and their display builds the equation up (12 + 34 + 56 comes back as 56, then 34 + 56, then 12 + 34 + 56). Swipe again before the last number comes into view: the numbers come in one after the other, in order.

Your phone does not show the mirror again until you tap a key, so the number does not come back on your screen. Random delay: 3 to 10 s, and the PeekSmith shows the delay that the phone selected. Use the practice phones in the Vanish tab to see the animations.

Ragdoll

Shake the phone: the numbers on the display fall and tumble with gravity. Tilt the phone to slide them. Shake again (or tap the display) to bring them back. With "Their phone too", the numbers on the spectator's phone fall at the same time. Settings are in the Vanish tab.

Peeks

Each number, total and result goes to your PeekSmith (Bluetooth in Aura), to PeekLink / Peek Island (online), and to your iPhone with Aura peer.

Aura peer mirror (top right button)

  • Double tap: this phone switches Spectator ↔ Magician. The other device switches too.
  • Tap: mirror on / off on the two devices. Mirror on makes this phone the Magician.
  • Your iPhone (Magician, in Aura Browser) shows the spectator's display live. The spectator's device (AuraClip) is Spectator.
  • The two devices must use the same room (default fvt-calc) and stay on screen.

Inject

The calculator sends a number to Inject (default: the last number typed) when the proximity sensor is covered, the phone is face down, or = is tapped (see Inject tab).

Problems

Not connected: open Peeks → Aura peer and read the Status and Peer test lines. "Aura.peer: —" means that Aura must be updated.
