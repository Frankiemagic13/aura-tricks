Help
1. Hold the π sign to open these settings.
2. Connect the PeekSmith, then tap Done.
3. The spectator taps a digit, holds a digit and drags to highlight a group, or finds a number (for example a birthday).
4. The PeekSmith shows the digits that come after the last chosen digit (10 by default).
5. If fewer digits are left at the end of the list, the PeekSmith shows the digits that are left.
