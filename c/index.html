1. Type the numbers with + between them. 2. While the last number is typed, peek at the clock button (top left): the hand points right = tap +, the hand points down = tap −. 3. The force starts on that + or −. Turn the phone face down and tap the screen. Each tap enters the next digit. Before you turn the phone, look at the time in the top left corner: its LAST DIGIT is the number of taps needed (for 10 taps it shows 0). 4. Turn the phone face up and tap = (or let it tap = for you). "Start after numbers" sets how many numbers come first.
