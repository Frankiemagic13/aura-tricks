Countdown buzz: before the routine starts, the PeekSmith buzzes this number of times. The light starts on the next beat after the last buzz, so you can count along. Press Play again during the countdown to stop it.
Record: tap Record routine (or the PeekSmith button set to Record). While recording, touch the screen = light on, lift your finger = light off. The PeekSmith buttons also work. Tap the screen with two fingers, or press the Record button again, to stop. The routine starts at the first light change. Play it with a PeekSmith button set to Play.
