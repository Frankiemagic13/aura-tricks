Room
My phone (control): this phone shows the control screen (phones, routines, PeekSmith). Spectator (flash): the black flashlight screen; its flash does what your phone sends. Use the same room on all phones (default fvt-calc). The role is the same as on the calculator and Notes.

Open and close

Hold the screen for 1 second to open these settings.

Light

  1. Tap the black screen one time. This prepares the light (iOS asks for camera access one time).
  2. Then the PeekSmith buttons turn the light on and off. If the light stops after the phone was locked, tap the screen one time.

Routine

  1. Tap Record routine. Touch the screen = light on, lift your finger = light off.
  2. Tap with two fingers (or the Record button) to stop.
  3. Play it with a PeekSmith button set to Play. Countdown buzz lets you count along before it starts.

Studio: several phones (Aura peer)

1. Setup

  1. Your phone: open this page in Aura Browser. Settings → Phones tab → This phone → My phone (control). The Studio opens. (If your phone is already the Magician on the calculator or Notes, it opens by itself.)
  2. Spectator phones: open this page in AuraClip. They show a black screen.
  3. Each phone gets a number (1, 2, 3…) in the order it connects.
  4. "Needs 1 tap": the spectator taps the black screen one time (iOS rule for the flash). Then the tile shows Ready. Tap your M tile one time too.
  5. Same room on all phones (default fvt-calc). Keep all pages on screen.
  6. Start: the screen shows M (your phone) + phone 1. No phones yet? You can still record: tap the tiles (they show "Waiting…") and record as normal. Tap + Add phone for more layers (up to 9). Hold the last empty tile (0.7 s) to remove it, for example to start with 2 phones. When the phones connect later, the routine plays on them by their numbers.

2. Buttons

On: tap = the selected phones stay on · hold = on while you hold
Flash: a short blink (0.2 s) on the selected phones
All on: every phone and M on at once (no need to select)
Off: the selected phones off · nothing selected = all off
Record / Stop: record a routine
?This help · the gear opens the settings

Tap a phone tile to select it (orange ring). Tap again to remove it. A tile glows when its flash is on.

3. Record a routine

  1. Tap Record (Phones tab) or + Record new (Routines tab).
  2. Select phones and use On, Flash and Off. Each phone has its own layer, with a bar for each light and a red playhead.
  3. Mistake? Undo removes the last action. Or tap a bar, then Delete bar. Preview plays it on the tiles only.
  4. Tap Stop. Type a name, select an icon, a PeekSmith button, Loop and the Countdown buzz. Tap Save.
  5. Cancel (top left) throws the recording away.

4. Run a routine

  1. Routines tab: each card shows a live preview of what it will do.
  2. Tap a card: the screen goes black, the countdown buzzes (phone and PeekSmith), the routine plays on all phones at the same time, then the list comes back.
  3. Tap the black screen to stop.
  4. Hold a card (0.6 s) to edit or delete it.

Ready-made routines (Ping-pong, Heartbeat, Wave, Countdown 3-2-1, Lightning storm, Morse: MAGIC, Chase finale, Strobe party) are in the list. They use phones 1–3 and M; a phone that is not connected is skipped. Edit or delete them like your own.

5. From the PeekSmith

PeekSmith tab: select a routine for a button (or set it in the Save sheet). Press the button to run it from your pocket. Press it again to stop.

6. Problems

  • Tile shows "Waiting…": that phone is not connected. Open the page on it and keep it on screen.
  • "Needs 1 tap" after the phone was locked: tap its black screen one time again.
  • Settings → Phones tab → Test shows each phone, ready or not, and its Bluetooth time.
