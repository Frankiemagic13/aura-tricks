Open and close

Hold the screen for 1 second to open these settings.

Light

  1. Tap the black screen one time. This prepares the light (iOS asks for camera access one time).
  2. Then the PeekSmith buttons turn the light on and off. If the light stops after the phone was locked, tap the screen one time.

Routine

  1. Tap Record routine. Touch the screen = light on, lift your finger = light off.
  2. Tap with two fingers (or the Record button) to stop.
  3. Play it with a PeekSmith button set to Play. Countdown buzz lets you count along before it starts.

Studio: several phones (Aura peer)

1. Setup

  1. Your phone: open this page in Aura Browser. Settings → Phones tab → This phone → My phone (control). The Studio opens. (If your phone is already the Magician on the calculator or Notes, it opens by itself.)
  2. Spectator phones: open this page in AuraClip. They show a black screen.
  3. Each phone gets a number (1, 2, 3…) in the order it connects.
  4. "Needs 1 tap": the spectator taps the black screen one time (iOS rule for the flash). Then the tile shows Ready. Tap your M tile one time too.
  5. Same room on all phones (default fvt-calc). Keep all pages on screen.
  6. Start: the screen shows M (your phone) + phone 1. No phones yet? You can still record: tap the tiles (they show "Waiting…") and record as normal. Tap + Add phone for more layers (up to 9). Tap the × on the last empty tile to remove it, for example to start with 2 phones. When the phones connect later, the routine plays on them by their numbers.

2. Buttons

Phone tile: tap = a short flash (0.2 s) on that phone · hold = on while you hold · use more fingers for more phones
Flash: tap = all phones flash · hold = all phones on while you hold
All on: tap = every phone and M on · tap again = all off
Record / Stop: record a routine
?This help · the gear opens the settings

Tap a phone tile = a short flash on that phone. Hold it = on while you hold. A tile glows when its flash is on.

3. Record a routine

  1. Tap Record (Phones tab) or + Record new (Routines tab).
  2. Tap and hold the phone tiles, or use Flash and All on. Each phone has its own layer, with a bar for each light and a red playhead.
  3. Mistake? Undo removes the last action. Or tap a bar, then Delete bar. Preview plays it on the tiles only.
  4. Tap Stop. Type a name, select an icon, a PeekSmith button, Loop and the Countdown buzz. Tap Save.
  5. Cancel (top left) throws the recording away.

4. Run a routine

  1. Routines tab: each card shows a live preview of what it will do.
  2. Tap a card: the screen goes black, the countdown buzzes (phone and PeekSmith), the routine plays on all phones at the same time, then the list comes back.
  3. Tap the black screen to stop.
  4. Hold a card (0.6 s) to edit or delete it.

⇅ Sort: tap to change between A–Z and Recent (the routine you used or made last is at the top). The list is A to Z by default. Tap ☆ on a card to add it to ★ Favorites (tap ★ again to remove it). The Favorites tab shows only those.

Start (top right): At once = the routine starts when you tap it. Face down, PeekSmith click or both = tap the routine, the screen goes black and waits; turn the phone face down on the table or click any PeekSmith button to start it. Tap the black screen to cancel. The PeekSmith shows "Ready: name" while it waits.

There are 39 ready-made routines, for example Ping-pong, Wave, Countdown, Police, The chosen one, Roulette, Teleport, Thunder, Grand reveal, Applause and Firework. Each one starts with 2 phones (M + 1). Tap + on the card to add a phone (up to M + 9) and − to remove one. The routine and its preview change to fit. A phone that is not connected is skipped. You can edit or delete them like your own routines.

5. From the PeekSmith

PeekSmith tab: select a routine for a button (or set it in the Save sheet). Press the button to run it from your pocket. Press it again to stop.

Light buttons (PeekSmith)

  • PeekSmith tab → Left / Right / Side button light: select a phone (or All phones, or M).
  • Tap the button = that light on / off. Hold = on while you hold, off when you let go.
  • Tap during a routine = pause (the lights stay as they are). Tap again = the routine goes on from that point. The PeekSmith shows "Paused" / "Playing".
  • While you record, light buttons are recorded too.

6. Problems

  • Tile shows "Waiting…": that phone is not connected. Open the page on it and keep it on screen.
  • "Needs 1 tap" after the phone was locked: tap its black screen one time again.
  • Settings → Phones tab → Test shows each phone, ready or not, and its Bluetooth time.
