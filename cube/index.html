How to use

  1. Solve the real cube.
  2. Wake the cube: turn a face until the light flashes. The page connects by itself (Auto connect). If not, tap Connect cube.
  3. First time only: tap Calibrate and do the guided calibration.
  4. The page reads the real mix when it connects, so the screen cube matches. If not, solve the cube and tap Set as solved.
  5. Turn the real cube. The virtual cube turns the same way.

Photo prediction: mix the cube and stop: after a few seconds with no turns the photo is made by itself (Calibrate → Photo prediction). Or press the volume button (Aura 8) and a photo of the cube as it is now goes to your Photos app through an iOS Shortcut. Set it up in Calibrate → Photo prediction.

Drag the cube to turn the view. Test with no cube: press U D L R F B (hold Shift for a counter-clockwise turn).

Hold the cube the way you set in Calibrate → View → Top color (default: yellow on top, green in front). If the cube does not show in the list, disconnect it in LightBlue first.

Works in Aura Browser, Chrome or Edge on Mac, and Bluefy on iPhone. Safari does not have Bluetooth.
